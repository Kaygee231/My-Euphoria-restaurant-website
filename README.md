# Euphoria — Restaurant, Bar & Car Wash Website

A responsive multi-page starter website with:

- Home page
- Menu page
  - Everyday breakfast
  - Platters
  - Tuesday burger deals
  - Thursday burger deals
  - Sunday burger deals
  - Bar menu
- Online pickup ordering
- No deliveries
- No online payment
- Pay at restaurant notice
- Table reservation page
- Car wash section
- Mobile responsive navigation

## Files

- `index.html`
- `menu.html`
- `order.html`
- `reservation.html`
- `carwash.html`
- `styles.css`
- `script.js`

## Important

The forms are currently front-end demo forms. They do not send real orders or reservations anywhere.

For a live website, connect the forms to your preferred backend, email service, database, WhatsApp workflow, or restaurant management system.

Replace the sample restaurant name, prices, operating hours, phone number, address, photos, and menu items with your real business information.

No online payment gateway has been added, as requested.
